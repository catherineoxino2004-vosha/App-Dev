
# My Favorite Series
## Squid Game 🦑🎮

**Squid Game** is a South Korean survival drama series where hundreds of people in debt join a mysterious competition.  
They play deadly children's games for a huge cash prize — but losing means death.

**Main Characters:**
- Seong Gi-hun (Player 456)
- Cho Sang-woo (Player 218)
- Kang Sae-byeok (Player 067)
- The Front Man

**Why I Love It:**
> The show is intense and emotional. It shows how desperation and greed can change people, and how kindness still exists even in dark situations.

**Genre:** Drama, Thriller, Survival  
**Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)
